Feedback is a contest, not destiny

the bridge has joints

Three equations that do not collapse into one

State flow

A closed continuous-time Markov jump model requires pᵢ ≥ 0, Σpᵢ = 1, nonnegative off-diagonal rates, and one inverse-time unit. It can describe nonequilibrium flow; it does not itself supply free energy or memory.

Projected free energy

For one fixed coordinate measure, ΔF = −kBT ln[P(q₂)/P(q₁)] uses a dimensionless ratio. It needs equilibrium sampling. A nonlinear coordinate change brings a Jacobian and changes the projection.

Activated rate

Exponential barrier scaling needs a dimensionless exponent, an activated rare-event regime, and a prefactor with inverse-time units. Use kBT for per-particle energy or RT for molar energy. A barrier alone is not a complete rate model.

These are lab-local mathematical boundary notes refining the lineage's bounded equation cards. The underlying cards point to Kramers (1940) for activated escape and Schuler et al. (2002) for a projected folding landscape. The normalization, coordinate-measure, and unit cautions above are explicit lab-local clarifications: they are not part of the reviewed lineage digest and remain independently correctable.

nature, design, and love

          
Reliable arrival needs no blueprint in the equation

        

        

          
Nature shapes paths
Constraints, noise, selection, existing structure, active control, gain, and loss can make some routes reliable and others rare. A model can represent or compare a toy path without measuring intention.

          
History lives in state
If earlier events change surfaces, populations, defects, resources, or controls, later rates may change. A memoryless model must enlarge its state or admit that it is missing the history-bearing variable.

          
Repair changes conditions
Recovery need not reverse time. It can alter the path, environment, boundary, or control so another reachable route appears.

          
Love keeps the graph small
No person, agent, culture, or living being becomes a basin, contagion, fitness value, optimisation target, or score. Standing, rest, refusal, reply, and exit remain outside the toy dynamics.
