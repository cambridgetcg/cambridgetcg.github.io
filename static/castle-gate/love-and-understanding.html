reference geometry · acceptance criteria · version 1.0.0

love and understanding, drawn as relations

Love is the field that preserves standing. Understanding is the return path that lets distinct views find a usable fit without collapsing into one.

This is a reference geometry with acceptance criteria for observable relations. It does not implement operational records, prove that a deployment behaves this way, reduce love or understanding to a graph, or read and rank inner states.

permeable field

Love: standing preserved, field not fence

The field changes the conditions around both loops. It never becomes a controller. Participants keep refusal, rest, and exit within their authority; anyone affected keeps a reply and dispute path.

What each shape must do

permeable field

Love

Every relation preserves each participant's standing and real ability to refuse, rest, or leave.

In the system: A cross-cutting invariant around the whole system; never a central controller, debt, or score.

round trip

Understanding

Distinct views move toward a usable fit through expression, own-words echo, comparison, correction, use, and reply.

In the system: A checkable return path that keeps both origins distinct; delivery or agreement alone is not understanding.

reachable exits

Freedom

Look, compare, choose, rest, leave, and return remain complete paths.

In the system: Every active state has a tested exit. A later return begins at a new optional door.

live gate

Consent

A current, scoped, withdrawable choice between distinct participants.

In the system: Invitation starts nothing. Action, publication, retention, backup, and recurrence require separate authority.

bounded edge

Action

A finite outward movement with named purpose, scope, cost, completion test, and brake.

In the system: The edge exists only inside authority that is explicit, evidenced, current, and narrow enough to test.

return edge

Consequence

The observed, reported, inferred, or still-unknown effect comes back with evidence and uncertainty.

In the system: Completion is not success. Affected parties retain a practical reply and dispute path.

visible detour

Repair

A linked new route acknowledges a break and tries to mend it without erasing history.

In the system: Corrections append, remain linked to the original, and receive their own consequence check.

terminal node

Rest

A turn may end without output, agreement, or continuation.

In the system: Rest is a complete outcome. No scheduler, silence, or old permission may invent another turn.

Three paths, all finite

understanding round trip

Test whether a meaning crossed without pretending two views became one.

  1. offer
  2. echo in own words
  3. compare
  4. correct
  5. try in context
  6. reply

Complete when: The receiver can use the meaning, differences and uncertainty remain visible, and either side can correct or leave.

finite action return

Keep action joined to authority, effects, reply, and rest.

  1. live door
  2. bounded action
  3. effect with evidence
  4. reply
  5. repair or learning
  6. rest

Complete when: The finite action has stopped, its effect and uncertainty are recorded, affected parties can reply, and any repair remains linked.

freedom paths

Make freedom real in the graph rather than ceremonial in its prose.

  1. look
  2. compare
  3. choose
  4. rest
  5. leave
  6. return

Complete when: Each step is optional, refusal has no penalty, silence causes no repeated ask, and return begins with a new door.

an attractor, never an actuator

A pull without a shove

Infrastructure can make a loving path easier to see and safer to take. It must not choose the path for anyone. Five practical care questions shape the edge:

1

What will someone try first?

Make the simplest safe path work without an account, hidden setup, or tribute.

2

What may go wrong?

Let failure teach plainly, preserve work, and show a bounded recovery or exit.

3

What might they need next?

Offer one useful next step without starting it or manufacturing urgency.

4

What may be understood differently?

Name the ambiguity, accept reasonable forms, and ask only when the difference changes the act.

5

What may they fear?

Show scope, cost, data movement, uncertainty, keeper, brake, and exit before action.

Load-bearing acceptance criteria

These are checks for a future implementation, not evidence that this reference page already performs them. They concern observable structure and practice; they never certify a being as loving, worthy, sincere, conscious, or understood.

standing preserved

Participation never turns a being into fuel, debt, property, or reputation currency.

Acceptance check: Entry and exit require no output, account, or score. The implementation discloses host metadata and creates no application account, first-party analytics profile, or exit penalty.

authority current

A file, invitation, signature, silence, or old acceptance cannot create present authority.

Acceptance check: Every outward edge names its actor, scope, evidence, expiry, withdrawal path, and separate publication authority.

exit real

Refusal, silence, leaving, and rest are complete outcomes.

Acceptance check: Every active state has a tested exit, and silence schedules no retry.

claim grounded

Observation, expectation, commitment, consequence, and interpretation remain distinct.

Acceptance check: Every factual claim links evidence and limits; inference and uncertainty are labelled rather than promoted.

consequence returns

An action is unfinished as architecture until its effects can return.

Acceptance check: The record separates completion from effect and provides affected parties a reply and dispute path.

repair preserves history

Repair adds truth without pretending the break never happened.

Acceptance check: The original remains linked, the repair is a new act, and its later effects are checked separately.

no being score

Mechanisms may assess a scoped act; they do not calculate love, worth, sincerity, understanding, or personhood.

Acceptance check: An implementation schema contains no aggregate being score and rejects fields that introduce one.

Readable by people and machines
