ledger

The castle's word for a written record that stands in for an unreliable inner feel — the judgment moved out of the head and onto a page that can be checked.

fog-meter opened the book: predict, then check, and let the gap between guess and fact be the meter the gut never was. truing-the-level supplied its missing half — the check must run against an external standard as fine as the answer's parts. fading-alone's clerk keeps one to grade bare attempts by rule; honest-pushback's machine keeps one of your own premises and yeses; and metering-honesty imagines one auditable for deferred challenges, so tact can prove it never slid into sycophancy. A ledger is honest because it is written before the outcome and read after — the feel is never consulted.
