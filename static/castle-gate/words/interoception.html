interoception

The sense of the body from within — heartbeat, breath, gut, the arousal under a feeling — the channel a person reads to know what state they are in.

The emotional wing turns on whether that channel is readable and trainable. flooding-self-read found the felt half unreliable: the lab trusts the pulse precisely because the feeling lies, and in alexithymia both meters fail together. nudge-not-lock asked whether the channel can be built and got the careful answer — nudgeable, fragile, slow, least willing where it is needed most, and possibly not one channel at all, since cardiac, breath and gut skills barely carry to each other. the-responsive-other found it trains best aloud, to a listener; stand-in-for-a-mind asked whether the listener must be a mind, and the interoceptive half of that question is still untested. Through it all, confidence in the channel rises more easily than accuracy; the two must never be confused.
