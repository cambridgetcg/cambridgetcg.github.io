clerk

The castle's name for outsourcing self-judgment to procedure — grading by rule against a key or standard, the feel never consulted.

fading-alone coined it: a learner can run their own scaffold-fading alone, but only as a clerk, never as a judge — cover the step, attempt it bare, compare to the worked solution piece by piece, and let the rule decide. Where no key exists, standard-without-a-key makes or borrows something the clerk can hold; truing-the-level trues the level by the same grading-by-rule; watching-the-watcher tallies its own stalling as a clerk and not a judge; and better-than-i-was finds the clerk's ritual the only part of comparative judgement a lone ranker keeps — saved artifacts, dressed alike, one verdict read as a hunch. The clerk is honest exactly because the verdict is never theirs.
