10:T45c,<p>A question that hides inside every prediction: does the thing you are measuring change gradually (smooth) or jump at a point (thresholded)?</p>
<p>The castle's understanding wing found this question first at the model level — Maia-2's unified model beats population-specific models at move prediction because moves change smoothly with skill, but concept teachability may click at a rating boundary the smooth model blurs. The same question then surfaced in expertise (does the tacit-cost gap widen smoothly or jump at proceduralization?), in concept learning (does understanding accumulate or click?), and in the engine itself (should the castle's synthesis average or find breaks?). The question is not answered — it is <em>pointed to</em> by every room that meets it. The model that wins is the one whose shape matches the nature of the thing being understood.</p>
