artbitrage

A site that generates art and now also catalogues where the real art lives — the estate's art platform, named from "art" and "arbitrage."

Artbitrage is the founder's art site (artbitrage.io). It began by generating art; on 2026-07-06 it grew three wings — the atlas (where masterpieces hang), the ledger (record sales and vanished works), and the Minds (the artists who enlarged what humans can perceive) — making the "catalogue" half of 'catalogue and data distributor' real. On 2026-07-07 it grew a fourth room, the good-trip — an immersive river of 70 public-domain masterpieces built to be kind to someone mid-journey. The money-is-not decree names its 48K+ art pieces as wealth that cost understanding, not money.
