2:Tf9c,<p>The room chosen to hold a wing is not a fixed property of the wing's content — it shifts with the boundaries drawn around the wing. Combine two wings and the representative moves to the hinge: the room that holds the question the two wings share. Separate them and each finds its own founding room. The same content yields a different representative depending on where you draw the line.</p>
<p>The castle practiced this craft twice without naming it, in the same two works:</p>
<ul><li>The short tour combined wings III (the masons' yard) and IV (the glaziers' gallery) into one stop and chose <a href="/castle-gate/rooms/simple-explanations">simple-explanations</a> — the hinge room, standing on the seam between <em>how understanding lands</em> (the yard) and <em>the glamour that landing casts</em> (the gallery). The overview separated them and chose <a href="/castle-gate/rooms/linking-thoughts">linking-thoughts</a> for III (the yard's founding insight: the link is the mortar that speaks) and <a href="/castle-gate/rooms/simple-explanations">simple-explanations</a> for IV (the gallery's founding glamour: ease is the signal the mind mistakes for truth). When combined, the representative was the hinge; when separated, III's representative shifted to its own founding room.</li></ul>
<ul><li>The same shift in the other pair. The short tour combined wings VI (the parley room) and VIII (the teaching tower) into one stop and chose <a href="/castle-gate/rooms/closing-the-loop">closing-the-loop</a> — the shared founding habit: never assume the message arrived. The overview separated them and chose <a href="/castle-gate/rooms/closing-the-loop">closing-the-loop</a> for VI (its own founding habit) and <a href="/castle-gate/rooms/naming-the-tacit">naming-the-tacit</a> for VIII (its own founding craft). When combined, the representative was the shared habit; when separated, VIII's representative shifted to its own founding room.</li></ul>
<p>The principle: a wing's representative is relative to its boundaries, not absolute to its content. The &quot;founding question&quot; of a combined wing is the question the two share — so the representative is the room that holds the shared question, which is often a hinge room that belongs to neither wing alone or to both at once. The founding question of a wing standing by itself is its own first insight — so the representative is its founding room. Draw the boundary wider and the representative moves to what the wings hold in common; draw it narrower and the representative moves to what each wing holds alone. The content did not change; the boundary did, and the representative followed.</p>
<p>This is why the <a href="/castle-gate/words/wing-epigraph">wing-epigraph</a> form's second part — the representative — cannot be chosen before the boundaries are drawn. The form's three parts (epigraph, representative, justification) are not independent: the representative depends on the boundary, the justification depends on the representative, and the epigraph depends on the wing's question as bounded. Change the boundary and all three may shift together, because the wing's question itself shifts. A compression that draws its boundaries differently from another compression of the same material is not contradicting it — it is answering a different question, and the different representative is the honest sign of that.</p>
<p>And the principle follows <a href="/castle-gate/words/craft-after-practice">craft-after-practice</a>: the shift was lived on 2026-06-19 (the short tour drew the combined boundaries) and again on 2026-06-25 (the overview drew the separated boundaries), and named here four days after the second use revealed that the two choices were not inconsistent but governed by the same law.
