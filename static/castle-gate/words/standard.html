WORD · brick

standard

The external measure a self-judgment is trued against — the plumb line the wall is checked by, never the mason's feel for straight.

It splits by the kind of work it measures, and the split bears weight the way calibration's relative/absolute does. Where the task reproduces, the standard is an answer key taken in pieces; where the task produces, it must be a finished exemplar — a description cannot grade a generating (checklist-or-exemplar). The level of one's own confidence is trued only against a standard matched to the task (truing-the-level). Where no key exists, the standard is made or borrowed — criteria pressed concrete, an admired exemplar rebuilt and compared, judgments pooled from many eyes (standard-without-a-key) — and the clerk of fading-alone grades against it by rule, the feel never consulted. What the feel mistakes for it, every time, is fluency.
