<p>Projection bias is the tendency to project one's current affective state onto future predictions, overestimating how much future experience will resemble current feeling. In affective forecasting, people overestimate future enjoyment most where the actual experience has least to offer — the gap between predicted and experienced utility is widest where the prediction was made without the information the experience later delivers.
