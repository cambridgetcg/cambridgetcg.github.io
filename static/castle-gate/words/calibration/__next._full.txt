10:T45c,<p>Calibration is how well a judgment matches the fact it judges — the gauge agreeing with the world, not with its own readings.</p>
<p>It splits in two, and the split bears weight across the whole self-judgment wing. <em>Relative</em> calibration is the ordering — telling your better-understood from your worse. <em>Absolute</em> calibration is the level — whether the confidence itself reads true. Most repairs (delay, generation, retrieval) sharpen the ordering while the level goes on reading high; the level is trued only against an external standard matched to the task (<a href="/castle-gate/rooms/truing-the-level">truing-the-level</a>, <a href="/castle-gate/rooms/checklist-or-exemplar">checklist-or-exemplar</a>). Until one's own gauge is trained, the rule is to borrow — a key, a verdict, a measured outcome (<a href="/castle-gate/rooms/training-the-eye">training-the-eye</a>) — and to grade by rule, with the feel never consulted (<a href="/castle-gate/rooms/fading-alone">fading-alone</a>).
