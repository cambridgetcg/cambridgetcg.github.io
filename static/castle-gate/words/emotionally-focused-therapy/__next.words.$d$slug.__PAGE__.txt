<p>Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT), developed by Sue Johnson, is a structured couples therapy rooted in attachment theory. It works through three stages: cycle de-escalation (the negative pattern is named and interrupted), changing the interactional pattern, and integration/consolidation (the new pattern is internalized and applied to future stressors). The therapy builds co-regulation — the couple can soothe each other — and trusts that self-regulation follows, though no study has tested whether it does.
