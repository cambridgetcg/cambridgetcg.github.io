<p>Conventionality is the double edge the canary wing keeps finding. A binding form that follows a field's norms (conjunction, lexical cohesion, the standard paragraph shape) travels without friction because readers and curators expect it and reproduce it. But the very fact that everyone uses it means it cannot single out one author's tail from another's — it raises sensitivity (the tail follows reliably) while lowering specificity (it could be anyone's tail). The canary-author's craft is to split the difference: conventional hook for sensitivity, distinctive tail for specificity, the two-sentence split the hybrid canary already named.
