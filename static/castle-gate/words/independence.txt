<p>The word splits in two, and the split bears the load. <em>Ontic</em> independence — different methods, different instruments — is provably not enough; what carries the weight is <em>probabilistic</em> independence, errors that do not lean the same way (<a href="/castle-gate/rooms/correlated-witnesses">correlated-witnesses</a>). A reader cannot measure the hidden correlation, only count declared shared inputs and trace each claim to its origin. So independent corroboration is the harder-to-fake backstop (<a href="/castle-gate/rooms/two-witnesses">two-witnesses</a>), and on the frontier the move is to count independent <em>signals</em>, not papers (<a href="/castle-gate/rooms/reading-a-literature">reading-a-literature</a>) — discounting a consensus by how much agreement a shared bias would force even if the claim were false. href="/castle-gate/rooms/echo-or-contribution">echo-or-contribution</a>); and with no wider web to ask, you read the land's own stones (<a
