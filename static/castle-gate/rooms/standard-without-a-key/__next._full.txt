10:T1353,<p>The worry has the literature's own backing: worked examples are documented only where algorithms apply — mathematics, physics, programming — and the method's scholars concede that for interpreting poems or leading people, solution steps resist being written down (read 2026-06-10 — <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worked-example_effect" rel="noopener">worked-example effect</a>). But <em>no key</em> does not mean <em>no standard</em>. Sadler's account of open fields says the standard exists and is tacit — a concept of quality living &quot;largely in unarticulated form, inside [experts'] heads&quot; — fuzzy, real, and carried by exemplars rather than solutions (read 2026-06-10 — <a href="https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/13562517.2023.2244439" rel="noopener">Sadler revisited</a>). A standard locked in heads is the tacit knowledge of <a href="/castle-gate/rooms/naming-the-tacit">naming-the-tacit</a>, and its lesson carries: what an analyst can distill becomes a criterion; what will not shrink to a nameable contrast must ride whole, inside an exemplar.</p>
<p>Three stand-ins, each a way of pressing that tacit standard into something the clerk of <a href="/castle-gate/rooms/fading-alone">fading-alone</a> can hold.</p>
<p>First, criteria made concrete before the judging starts. Self-assessment of open work must be guided by explicit criteria, and specificity decides everything: &quot;introduces herself to the patient&quot; trains a checkable eye where &quot;shows empathy&quot; leaves you grading vibes (read 2026-06-10 — <a href="https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/education/articles/10.3389/feduc.2019.00087/full" rel="noopener">Andrade 2019</a>).</p>
<p>Second, the manufactured key. Franklin's method: study a piece you admire, rebuild it from memory, lay yours beside the original, study the differences. The admired work has become a worked solution (read 2026-06-10 — <a href="https://commoncog.com/the-problems-with-deliberate-practice/" rel="noopener">commoncog</a>).</p>
<p>Third, comparison with no key anywhere. Adaptive comparative judgement ranks essays and designs by nothing but repeated &quot;which of these two is better,&quot; reaching reliabilities of 0.85–0.96 — the standard emerges from many judgments, not a page (read 2026-06-10 — <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adaptive_comparative_judgement" rel="noopener">ACJ</a>, <a href="https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/education/articles/10.3389/feduc.2026.1755046/full" rel="noopener">Frontiers 2026</a>). But it takes many judges, and consistent is not correct: quick comparisons reward surface polish over substance.</p>
<p>What binds all three is other people, early. Across 160 studies, self-scores already track expert scores moderately; given feedback, the overestimation shrinks to insignificance (read 2026-06-10 — <a href="https://ernestopanadero.es/Publications/Articles/078_Leon_et_al_2023_How_accurate_ar_our_students.pdf" rel="noopener">León, Panadero &amp; García-Martínez 2023</a>). The key can be internalized — by being graded enough times by eyes that hold it; where a field has no training methods at all, Ericsson said plainly: it takes a coach (read 2026-06-10 — <a href="https://ysamphy.com/anders-ericsson-deliberate-practice/" rel="noopener">Ericsson summary</a>). And no key was ever sufficient alone — handed objective measures, only 18.5% of 426 medical students graded themselves accurately (read 2026-06-10 — <a href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11515314/" rel="noopener">PMC</a>).</p>
<p>So self-fading survives the missing key. The key is made — criteria pressed concrete, an exemplar rebuilt — or borrowed, and the scaffold that fades last is the other person.</p>
<h2>What stays uncertain</h2>
<p>uncertain: no study hands learners the fading itself in an ill-defined domain and watches; the self-assessment meta-analysis carried very high heterogeneity (I² = 97%); ACJ's reliability edge over rubrics is contested (Bramley &amp; Vitello 2016), and its high reliability can sit atop low validity — ranking consistently on the wrong qualities. How short a feedback loop can be engineered for essays and designs remains an open research question.</p>
