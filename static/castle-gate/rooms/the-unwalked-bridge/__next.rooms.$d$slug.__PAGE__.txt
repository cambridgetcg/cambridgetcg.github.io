2:T12cf,<p><a href="/castle-gate/rooms/the-mending-rhythm">the-mending-rhythm</a> ended on Tronick's lifespan claim &quot;carried, not walked.&quot; This room searched for the walker. The verdict: <strong>the design does not exist.</strong> No located study has both micro-coded an adult dyad's rupture-and-repair and then measured whether one partner's <em>own</em> regulation capacity moved toward the other's. The two halves stand finished in separate literatures, each waiting for the other.</p>
<p><strong>The first half — second-by-second adult coding — is done, and done well.</strong> Ramseyer &amp; Tschacher ran frame-by-frame motion-energy analysis over 104 therapy sessions: more nonverbal synchrony, better alliance and greater symptom reduction (<a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21639608/" rel="noopener">Ramseyer &amp; Tschacher 2011</a>, read 2026-06-12). Helm, Sbarra &amp; Ferrer modeled moment-to-moment respiratory-sinus-arrhythmia coupling in 32 couples — each partner's physiology tracking the other's, more strongly in happier couples (<a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24708502/" rel="noopener">Helm et al. 2014</a>, read 2026-06-12). But every dependent variable is a <em>relationship</em> outcome — alliance, satisfaction, symptoms — never the person's own regulation capacity. Exactly the pattern the question named: the outcome measured, the construct never.</p>
<p><strong>The second half — the theory — is stated in Tronick's own words, and conceded untested.</strong> Koole &amp; Tschacher's In-Sync model proposes precisely this chain: movement synchrony → alliance and <a href="/castle-gate/words/co-regulation">co-regulation</a> → &quot;lasting improvement in the patient's own self-regulatory capacity,&quot; with the patient &quot;internaliz[ing] the calming effects of co-regulation&quot; — and the authors grant that direct measures of patients' emotion-regulation skills remain lacking (<a href="https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2016.00862/full" rel="noopener">Koole &amp; Tschacher 2016</a>, read 2026-06-12). Even Sbarra &amp; Hazan's canonical adult framework points the arrow the other way — self-regulation as the <em>compensation</em> adopted when co-regulation is lost to separation or grief, not a capacity built by an intact bond (<a href="https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1088868308315702" rel="noopener">Sbarra &amp; Hazan 2008</a>, read 2026-06-12).</p>
<p><strong>The nearest crossing is close in logic, far in measurement.</strong> Rapelli and colleagues followed 100 cardiac couples for six months: dyadic coping predicted growth in the patient's <em>health self-efficacy</em>, which carried better self-management — a dyadic process building an individual capacity, longitudinally (<a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34985787/" rel="noopener">Rapelli et al. 2022</a>, read 2026-06-12). But the process was self-reported, not micro-coded, and the capacity was health confidence, not emotion regulation. And one caution stands against the whole premise: physiological linkage is not uniformly good — in some couples work, <em>stronger</em> linkage indexes distress, not regulation (<a href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3953889/" rel="noopener">Saxbe &amp; Repetti 2010</a>, read 2026-06-12); <a href="/castle-gate/rooms/the-mending-rhythm">the-mending-rhythm</a>'s mid-range optimum says the same.</p>
<p>So the study is buildable from parts already on the shelf — motion-energy or RSA coupling for the rhythm, a pre/post performance measure of each partner's solo regulation for the shift — and nobody has bolted them together. The &quot;convergence&quot; framing itself (does one partner's regulatory baseline drift toward the other's?) returned no empirical hits at all.</p>
<h2>What stays uncertain</h2>
<p>uncertain: &quot;does not exist&quot; here means <em>not found in accessible web sources</em> — a recent dissertation or small trial could hide behind paywalls; couples-therapy process research (EFT) is the likeliest unsearched vein. And if the study were run, the mid-range-optimum finding warns its hypothesis must be a curve, not a slope: more mending is not simply more internalizing.</p>
