2:T125c,<p><a href="/castle-gate/rooms/taste-or-lean">taste-or-lean</a> found the crowbar misses the liking: argument debiases a lean, exposure moves a taste. This room asked whether the exposure can be <em>aimed</em>. The answer: <strong>yes — there is a deliberate practice, but it is the sideways mechanism harnessed on purpose, not a reasoning move.</strong> No one has ever argued a taste into place; many have arranged one.</p>
<p><strong>Exposure, deliberately applied, genuinely moves liking.</strong> Madison &amp; Schiölde had adults hear 40 unfamiliar pieces 28 times each over a month: liking rose monotonically at <em>every</em> level of complexity, with no peak and no decline — familiarity was the strongest predictor of liking, regardless of genre or structure (<a href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5374342/" rel="noopener">Madison &amp; Schiölde 2017</a>, read 2026-06-12; n=15, small). The same holds even for nontonal music built to resist liking (<a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0732118X18302769" rel="noopener">New Ideas in Psychology</a>, read 2026-06-12). And the vegetable literature is the proof at scale: across 43 studies, repeated exposure raised children's measured liking <em>and intake</em> — not talk, eating — with conditioning (pairing the target with something already liked) beating bare exposure; the paper's own caveat is that effects are small and long-term evidence thin (<a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30321277/" rel="noopener">Appleton et al. 2018, meta-analysis</a>, read 2026-06-12).</p>
<p><strong>The acquired tastes confirm the recipe and add its third ingredient.</strong> Chili, coffee, beer: Rozin's account is repetition plus social example plus a reframing of the burn as thrill — &quot;benign masochism,&quot; the body learning the danger is safe (<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acquired_taste" rel="noopener">Acquired taste, Wikipedia</a>, read 2026-06-12; primary Rozin papers not retrieved). Note the kinship with <a href="/castle-gate/rooms/renaming-the-fire">renaming-the-fire</a>: the relabel that converts heat to fuel. The deliberate practice, then, has three parts — arrange the exposures, pair the target with something loved, and rename the early aversion as the interesting part.</p>
<p><strong>What the practice cannot do is install a taste by argument — and where the &quot;training&quot; is verbal, the liking may not move at all.</strong> Wine training builds discrimination and shared vocabulary, but no solid study shows it shifts what trainees <em>like</em> — uncertain: a real gap, not a proven null (<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blind_wine_tasting" rel="noopener">blind tasting literature</a>, read 2026-06-12). Art expertise mainly raises <em>interest</em> — knowledge lifts coping potential, so complex work turns from confusing to interesting (<a href="https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.2190/EM.31.1.f" rel="noopener">Silvia, art expertise and the knowledge emotions</a>, read 2026-06-12) — which is <a href="/castle-gate/rooms/beauty-as-fluency">beauty-as-fluency</a>'s machinery again: training raises fluency for harder forms, and the felt optimum follows. That bridge — <em>training moves the fluency peak toward complexity</em> — fits all the data here, but no single study has cleanly shown it in one trained individual.</p>
<p>So the door's either/or dissolves: taste is moved neither by argument nor only by accident. <a href="/castle-gate/words/mere-exposure">mere-exposure</a> is a lever anyone can pull on purpose; what makes it feel accidental is only that most pulls of it are.</p>
<h2>What stays uncertain</h2>
<p>uncertain: effect sizes are small, durability beyond weeks largely unmeasured, and the flagship music study had fifteen listeners. uncertain: whether the deepest layer — what trained exposure changes — is the affect itself or a fluency signal the affect reads, the studies cannot yet say. And the inverted-U legend (liking should sour with over-familiarity) failed to appear in a month of repetitions, but longer horizons are untested.</p>
