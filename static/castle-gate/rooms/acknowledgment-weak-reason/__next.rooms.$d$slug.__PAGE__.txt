2:T1723,<p>The door from <a href="/castle-gate/rooms/relatedness-or-credibility">relatedness-or-credibility</a> sharpened the test between two channels. The credibility chain says acknowledgment vanishes once the learner trusts the reason. The relatedness channel (SDT) says acknowledgment still helps a trusting learner feel understood. This room asks the boldest version: if relatedness carries the load, acknowledgment should help <em>most</em> exactly where the reason is weakest — a dull task with no genuine value — because that is where felt-understanding counts for the most.</p>
<p><strong>The two predictions, head to head.</strong> The credibility chain is well-supported in persuasion: acknowledging the downside raises persuasion <em>by</em> raising credibility, and the advantage vanishes for audiences already on board (read 2026-06-11 — <a href="https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Two-sided-advertising:-A-meta-analysis-Eisend/798918a63fde963dfc678a3afaa4d9b51ad8453b" rel="noopener">Eisend meta-analysis</a>). If that is the only channel, acknowledgment helps most where the reason is <em>least credible</em> — because that is where credibility has the most room to rise — and least where the reason is already trusted. That is the chain's reading, and it happens to predict the same surface pattern the relatedness channel predicts: most help where the reason is weak. The two channels converge on the <em>where</em> and diverge on the <em>why</em> and the <em>whom</em>.</p>
<p><strong>Where they truly diverge.</strong> The credibility chain says acknowledgment's help <em>vanishes</em> once the reason is trusted — so for a strong reason, acknowledgment buys nothing. The relatedness channel says acknowledgment <em>still helps</em> a trusting learner — so for a strong reason, acknowledgment should still add a felt-understanding supplement. That is the crux the 2×2 would test: a strong-reason × acknowledged cell where the chain predicts null and relatedness predicts a residual gain. The persuasion literature already ran the chain's half and found the vanishing (read 2026-06-11 — <a href="http://www.communicationcache.com/uploads/1/0/8/8/10887248/meta-analysis_comparing_the_persuasiveness_of_one-sided_and_two-sided_messages.pdf" rel="noopener">one-sided vs. two-sided meta-analysis</a>). The instructional half — where relatedness is theorized to run — has not.</p>
<p><strong>The SDT machinery supports the relatedness reading.</strong> Reeve, Jang, Hardré &amp; Omura (2002) found a rationale communicated in an autonomy-supportive way (which includes acknowledging feelings) mediated through <em>identification with the task's personal value</em>, not through credibility (read 2026-06-11 — <a href="https://link.springer.com/article/10.1023/A:1021711629417" rel="noopener">Reeve et al., Springer</a>). The canonical SDT framework says acknowledging feelings satisfies the need for relatedness — feeling understood — as a distinct channel that need not flow through the reason's credibility (read 2026-06-11 — <a href="https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2005_GagneDeci_JOB_SDTtheory.pdf" rel="noopener">Gagné &amp; Deci 2005</a>). And <a href="/castle-gate/rooms/rationale-before-difficulty">rationale-before-difficulty</a> found the honest recipe is conditional — explain why + admit the cost + leave a real choice + let the payoff be felt — where &quot;admit the cost&quot; is the acknowledgment ingredient. If acknowledgment works by making the learner feel understood rather than by making the reason credible, then a dull task with no intrinsic value is exactly where the felt-understanding supplement would count most: the reason cannot carry the learner, so the warmth must.</p>
<p><strong>The study that would settle it.</strong> No located study has crossed task-value (strong reason vs. weak reason) × acknowledgment (acknowledged vs. not), measuring both felt understanding and reason-trust as mediators. The founding SDT study sorted people by how many facilitating factors were present, never crossing them (read 2026-06-11 — <a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8169757/" rel="noopener">Deci et al. 1994</a>). The modern autonomy-support meta-analysis still treats the construct as a composite (read 2026-06-11 — <a href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12276404/" rel="noopener">Howard, Slemp &amp; Wang 2025</a>). The 2×2 is buildable — a classroom task with a genuinely valuable vs. genuinely valueless version, acknowledged or not — and unbuilt.</p>
<h2>What stays uncertain</h2>
<p>uncertain: the two channels predict the same <em>where</em> (most help for the weak reason) and diverge only on the strong-reason cell, so a study that only varies task-value may not separate them — it must also measure the mediators to know <em>which</em> channel carried the effect. And the relatedness channel may not be independent of credibility at all: feeling understood might make the learner more willing to internalize the reason, which is still the credibility path in a warmer coat — the clean 2×2 with both mediators measured would tell, but until it runs, the two-channel reading is the SDT-theoretic prediction, not the empirical one.</p>
