2:T146f,<p>Split the tutor's verdict into its jobs and the question answers itself unevenly. The tutor does three things: chooses which step to bare next, witnesses the bare attempt, and grades it. Handed all three at once, learners do them badly — a meta-analysis of learner control in educational technology found an overall effect near zero (g = 0.05; read 2026-06-10 — <a href="https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1034262" rel="noopener">Karich, Burns &amp; Maki 2014</a>); in real tutoring systems, 72% of help actions were unproductive, split between clicking to the bottom-out hint and refusing help while drowning (read 2026-06-10 — <a href="https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~bmclaren/pubs/AlevenEtAl-HelpSeeking-ITS2004.pdf" rel="noopener">Aleven et al., Toward Tutoring Help Seeking</a>); and left to grade their own recall, students scored wrong answers as right, dropped items too early, and measurably learned less for it (read 2026-06-10 — <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0959475211000685" rel="noopener">Dunlosky &amp; Rawson 2012</a>).</p>
<p>But the jobs differ. The first two survive solitude. The cover test of <a href="/castle-gate/rooms/when-the-trade-flips">when-the-trade-flips</a> <em>is</em> the tutor's own criterion — a step fades once produced without help (read 2026-06-10 — <a href="http://www.cee.uma.pt/ron/Salden%20et%20al.%20-%20The%20Expertise%20Reversal%20Effect%20and%20Worked%20Examples.pdf" rel="noopener">Salden et al.</a>) — self-administered. Production is the honest part. What rots alone is the grading, and the research names the rot precisely: <em>absence of standards</em>. Learners judging their answers while inspecting the correct one judged far more accurately, and breaking the standard into idea units and checking each one cut the overconfidence on wrong-but-close answers (read 2026-06-10 — <a href="https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1080/17470218.2010.502239" rel="noopener">Dunlosky, Hartwig, Rawson &amp; Lipko 2011</a>). Judge late, besides — delayed self-judgments discriminate known from unknown far better than judgments made in the warmth of study (read 2026-06-10 — <a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21219059/" rel="noopener">Rhodes &amp; Tauber 2011</a>).</p>
<p>So the solo learner never replaces the tutor's judgment with the inner feel <a href="/castle-gate/rooms/fog-meter">fog-meter</a> discredited. They replace it with the tutor's <em>procedure</em> plus the tutor's answer pressed into paper: cover one step, attempt it bare, compare the attempt to the worked solution piece by piece, and let a rule — not a vote — decide. Matched the key: that plank comes down. Failed: the plank goes back, no debate. It is fog-meter's predict-then-check ledger turned to <a href="/castle-gate/words/scaffolding">scaffolding</a>, and the borrowed calibration of <a href="/castle-gate/rooms/training-the-eye">training-the-eye</a> by another name. Even running this protocol is trainable — students who watched a model self-assess and pick the next task got better at both, and learned more (read 2026-06-10 — <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0959475211000697" rel="noopener">Kostons, van Gog &amp; Paas 2012</a>).</p>
<p>Yes, then — alone, but only as a clerk, never as a judge. Honesty is outsourced to the key and the rule; the feel is never consulted, only trained on the side.</p>
<h2>What stays uncertain</h2>
<p>uncertain: no study found hands learners control of the <em>fading itself</em> and measures whether they run it honestly — the near-zero meta covers learner control broadly, and the idea-unit and ledger fixes are lab-scale and short. Whether rule-following survives weeks of solo motivation, with the clerk and the learner one tired person, is untested. And the whole protocol presupposes a worked solution to grade against — where no key exists, this answer stops and <a href="/castle-gate/rooms/standard-without-a-key">standard-without-a-key</a> carries on: the standard is made or borrowed, criteria pressed concrete, an admired exemplar rebuilt, judgments pooled from many eyes. The nearest test kept in this room is <a href="/castle-gate/rooms/reread-or-refamiliar">reread-or-refamiliar</a>'s: step away and let delayed blank-page generation judge what no key can.</p>
