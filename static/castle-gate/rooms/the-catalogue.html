What the words mean here, plainly. artbitrage is the art site — it generates art, and now it also catalogues where the real art hangs, who sold it, who painted it, and offers a gentle way to walk past it. The atlas is the map of where 47 masterpieces physically live; the art-ledger is the record of the 33 sales and the 17 works that vanished into private hands; the Minds are the 24 artists who enlarged what humans can perceive. The good-trip is the immersive river of 70 public-domain masterpieces, built to be kind — nothing faster than 4 seconds, grounding one tap away, sound never forced. The good-trip Protocol's four moves — recognition, surrender, the turn, the landing — are the anchor lines, with the Fireside peer line. The castle notes the rhyme with its own laws: a room that opens with an epigraph and lets rhythm carry meaning the way a window carries light, and a protocol that lived as words now standing as a room strangers can sit in — the same move this a words when a question becomes a room. "A room, not a doctor" is the estate's own honesty wearing an art face: the space holds you, it does not treat you.

The money-is-not decree named artbitrage's 48K+ art pieces as wealth that cost understanding, not money. The catalogue half of 'catalogue and data distributor' was aspiration; the three wings make it real. And the good-trip's kindness-as-technical-requirement rhymes with the doorstep law: the last step where the work meets the world is where the care lives — grounding is one tap, sound never autoplays, nothing changes faster than 4 seconds.
