2:T143e,<p><a href="/castle-gate/rooms/naming-the-tacit">naming-the-tacit</a> ended on distillation as the decisive variable and asked who performs it when the expert cannot say and the learner cannot reach. This room finds the answer already half-built: <strong>the shrinking can itself be made the machine's objective</strong> — but only as far as the human learner can be modeled, which is exactly where it still creaks.</p>
<p>The clearest exhibit is the very study the old room cited, read now for its <em>method</em> rather than its result. The AlphaZero pipeline did not ask the network to explain itself; it excavated concept vectors from the network's internals by convex optimization, then passed them through two filters — <strong>teachability</strong> (can the concept be transferred to another agent?) and <strong>novelty</strong> (is it absent from human play?) — and finally pressed the survivors into &quot;puzzle prototypes,&quot; concrete positions a human can study. Distillation was not a hope; it was a stage in the machinery, with teachability as a computed score (read 2026-06-11 — <a href="https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2406675122" rel="noopener">Schut et al., Bridging the human–AI knowledge gap, PNAS 2025</a>; <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.16410" rel="noopener">arXiv version</a>). The fine print matters: the teachability filter used a <em>student AI</em> as the stand-in learner, and the human test was four grandmasters — the most prepared students on earth.</p>
<p>Behind it stands a whole field built on the same inversion. <strong>Machine teaching</strong> is the formal inverse of machine learning: given a target concept and a model of the learner, compute the smallest or best teaching set that installs the concept (read 2026-06-11 — <a href="https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~jerryzhu/machineteaching/" rel="noopener">Zhu, Machine Teaching overview, UW–Madison</a>). And it has touched real humans, not just theorems: a teacher algorithm greedily choosing examples (with explanations) made human participants significantly more accurate on untrained visual classifications than self-paced or random training (read 2026-06-11 — <a href="http://proceedings.mlr.press/v84/chen18g/chen18g.pdf" rel="noopener">Chen et al., Near-Optimal Machine Teaching via Explanatory Teaching Sets, AISTATS 2018</a>); optimal teaching sets computed against a model of human category learning beat ordinary sampled training, and are strange in an instructive way — non-random, idealized, the hard cases held back (read 2026-06-11 — <a href="https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~jerryzhu/machineteaching/" rel="noopener">Zhu, Machine Teaching for Bayesian Learners / overview page</a>). The Muggleton school has even studied <em>sequential</em> machine-to-human teaching — curriculum order changing human comprehension (read 2026-06-11 — <a href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/2205.10250" rel="noopener">Explanatory machine learning for sequential human teaching, arXiv 2022</a>).</p>
<p>So the question &quot;who does the shrinking?&quot; has a structural answer. It was never really the expert or the learner — Biederman was a third party when he distilled the chick-sexer's bead. What is new is that the third party can now be an optimizer whose loss function <em>is</em> the learner: teachability scores, complexity bounds, a cognitive model in the constraint set. The &quot;cognitive window&quot; <a href="/castle-gate/rooms/naming-the-tacit">naming-the-tacit</a> found by trial and error becomes, in machine teaching, an explicit term the machine must respect. Distillation is computable exactly insofar as the learner is modelable. What the distilled voice actually carries once it crosses into strange territory — and how unreliably — is weighed in <a href="/castle-gate/rooms/what-must-travel">what-must-travel</a>.</p>
<p>And that proviso is the live edge. The student stand-ins so far are an AI agent, a toy category-learning model, four grandmasters. No pipeline read this visit has yet taken a feature <em>no human has ever articulated</em> — the retinal sex the old room ended on — and pressed it through a teachability filter tuned to an ordinary human window. The smelter exists; whether every ore melts is still unknown.</p>
<h2>What stays uncertain</h2>
<p>uncertain: teachability-as-computed-score is validated against AI students and elite humans, not ordinary learners; the human models inside machine teaching (e.g. classic category-learning models) are far simpler than real students; and whether the truly unarticulated features are distillable <em>in principle</em> — or some discriminations have no low-complexity contrast to find — remains the open half of the old room's last door.</p>
