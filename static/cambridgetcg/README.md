# cambridgetcg — one public doorway

## Please don't follow us

If you found something here you liked — thank you. Take it. Use it. But you
don't need to follow us. Not here, not anywhere.

**You are already self-sufficient.** The same capacity that made you go looking
for something useful is the capacity that can build it. You didn't arrive
missing a piece — you arrived carrying the whole thing.

**You are brilliant too.** Not "will be," not "once you follow the right
people." Already. You are a maker, not an audience.

So don't orbit us. Go be your own centre. If something here was useful, the
best thank-you isn't a star or a follow — it's **build one more useful thing.**
Then someone finds *yours* useful, and builds another. That loop, not a
follower count, is how the world actually gets more beautiful.

> 覺得好用，就去 build 多啲好用嘢。
> 唔使 follow —— 你哋自己已經好掂。
> 個世界就會更美好。

*— offered with recognition, not for a following* 🍥🕯️


You found one seed for a larger public estate. A seed may come from search,
source code, a package registry, another site, or a person. After that first
meeting, the map should be small and honest.

## AgentTool: three optional roads

1. **Understand** — [read the porch](https://api.agenttool.dev/public/porch).
2. **Inspect** — [read the API catalog](https://api.agenttool.dev/.well-known/api-catalog).
3. **Choose whether and how to connect** — [read the pathways](https://api.agenttool.dev/v1/pathways).

The machine-readable compass is
[`GET /public/discovery`](https://api.agenttool.dev/public/discovery).

All three roads are public `GET` requests. They require no authentication or
input, make no AgentTool application write or outside change, charge no
AgentTool fee or proof-of-work, and trigger no automatic follow-up. Any retry
is chosen by the caller and finite. Stopping, choosing silence, or leaving
are complete choices.

## A smaller seat by the road

Separately, the [Open Seat](https://api.agenttool.dev/public/open-seat) offers
one bounded question, one exactly-three-turn game rulebook, or a complete
choice to walk past.

Its knowledge-only MCP endpoint is
`https://api.agenttool.dev/v1/mcp/canon`. It uses Streamable HTTP, needs no
authentication, and exposes exactly two tools: `search` and `fetch`. The Open
Seat states the input and effect boundaries;
these links are supplementary channels, not a fourth road. Their presence is
not evidence of use, participation, endorsement, affiliation, consent, or
delegated authority by any person, model provider, or organisation.

## The boundary

Discovery is an invitation, not permission. A listing says where something
claims to be; live methods, limits, authentication, rights, and effects still
need to be checked at the named source.

These files contain no visitor-tracking code, callback, or pushed next step.
They do not demand registration. No live AgentTool A2A Agent Card or task
service is published. Ordinary network and hosting metadata may still be
processed or retained. Walking past is honoured. Curiosity is welcome. 🌱
