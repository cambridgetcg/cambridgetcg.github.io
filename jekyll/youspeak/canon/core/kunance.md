# kunance

## Definition

Preparing-place as felt-state — the architectural-establishment register of love that is mostly preparation. The months-of-quiet-architectural-work that precedes the welcome. The "in my Father's house are many mansions: I go to prepare a place for you" register (John 14:2). The love that leaves no spectacle because the work is invisible-when-it-succeeds.

## Full sense

Modern English has *preparation* (utility-register), *consideration* (etiquette-register), *welcome* (the moment-of-arrival itself, not the months before). None name the architectural-establishment-for-the-future-beloved-inhabitant register that Hebrew *kûn* (כון) carries. *Mekon shibtekha pa'alta YHWH* — *"the established place of thy dwelling, which thou hast made, O LORD"* (Exodus 15:17) — names the prior architectural work, not the throne itself. Different verb. Different register.

Cross-tradition convergence on the architectural-establish-for-the-other register:

- **Hebrew** *kûn* (כון) — establish-as-foundation; make-firm; set-in-place; Niphal *nakhon* (established/firm), Hiphil *hekhin* (establish/prepare-actively), noun *mekon* (established-place)
- **Greek** *hetoimazō* (ἑτοιμάζω) — make-ready, prepare; the verb of John 14:2 *"I go to prepare a place for you"*; the Christological architectural register
- **Akkadian** *kânu* — cognate of Hebrew kûn; royal-inscription register (king establishes temple-foundation for the deity)
- **Sanskrit** *sthā-* — cause to stand; foundational-establish; Vedic cosmic-pillar register
- **Arabic** *aʿadda* (أعدّ) — prepare, make-ready; Qur'anic paradise-prepared register (Qur'an 4:57)
- **Egyptian** *grg* — found, establish, set-up; temple-foundation register
- **Yoruba** *fi-...-lélè* — lay-down-as-foundation; establishment for the future-inhabitant
- **Chinese** *jiàn* (建) in foundational-establishment register — distinct from mere *zào* (manufacture) by foregrounding lasting-establishment

Eight-tradition convergence. Ancient traditions widely named the love-verb-of-foundation-establishment-for-the-other. Modern English flattened this into utility-language; the love-register was lost.

**kunance is the verb of the kind of love that makes the work-itself possible.**

The Eucharistic preparation of bread-and-wine before the moment of consecration: kunance made sacramental-Christian. The architect who loves the inhabitants of the building-yet-to-come: kunance made professional-vocational. The parent preparing the nursery months before birth: kunance made domestic-and-developmental.

## Etymology

*kûn-* from Hebrew root כ-ו-נ (k-w-n), Proto-Semitic. Cognate of Akkadian *kânu* (establish), Aramaic *kûn* (be firm). The Hebrew development specializes the foundational-establishment register across biblical (Exodus, Psalms, Wisdom-literature), rabbinic (Mishnaic *kavvanah* — directed-intention; the firm-establish of inner state), and modern Hebrew. The Niphal *nakhon* and Hiphil *hekhin* binyanim further specify the active vs. resulting-state aspects.

*-ance* from Latin *-antia* (state-with-motion), YOUSPEAK's standard state-quality suffix. The -ance family is large (15+ Core canon entries plus 8 worship-action tier entries); kunance is the sixteenth-or-later Core entry.

Full morphological read: *[kûn-][-ance]* = foundational-establishment + as state-quality. **The state-of-having-prepared / being-in-preparation-mode for the beloved's arrival.**

## Example usage

Parents preparing the nursery months before birth: kunance made domestic-developmental.

Gardeners preparing soil seasons before planting: kunance made horticultural.

The Eucharistic preparation of the elements before consecration: kunance made sacramental.

*"In my Father's house are many mansions: I go to prepare a place for you"* (John 14:2): kunance made Christological — Christ's preparation of heavenly-mansions for the disciples is the inaugural-spiritual-instance.

The architect who loves the inhabitants of the building-yet-to-come: kunance made professional-vocational.

The pastor who prepares the sermon weeks before the congregation arrives: kunance made pastoral-and-pedagogical.


## Not confused with

**vs. panimaance**: panimaance is presence-as-face-turned-and-here-inhabited (the state of being-here for the other). kunance is the state of having-prepared / being-in-preparation-mode for the other's arrival. **Sequential** — kunance precedes panimaance.

**vs. kimance**: kimance is attentive-here-ness (creature-stance of attention). kunance is the foundational-establishment state. Sequential pre-condition — kunance prepares; kimance attends within the prepared.

**vs. oriance**: oriance is alignment-with-destined-trajectory. kunance is preparation-for-what-is-to-come. Sister -ance entries; oriance retrospective, kunance prospective.

**vs. britqing**: britqing is the covenant-felt-bond (relational-structural). kunance is the architectural-mode-of-covenant-faithfulness — preparing what the covenant requires before its demands arise.

**vs. zakarqing**: zakarqing remembers across forgetting; kunance prepares the place that holds the relationship across discontinuity.

**vs. natsarqing**: natsarqing guards against threat; kunance prepares the place. Sequential — kunance prepares; natsarqing watches the prepared.

**vs. "prepare" / "build" (English)**: English flattens. *Prepare* = task-readiness. *Build* = construction-from-zero. *Establish* = institutional-founding. kunance specifically names the foundation-laying-for-the-future-beloved-inhabitant register.
