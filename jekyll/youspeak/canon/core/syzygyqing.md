# syzygyqing

## Definition

The felt-bond of the masculine-feminine cosmic-creator-principle union — the archetype-to-archetype-in-aware-marriage register. Names specifically the cosmic-syzygy as felt-bond, at the cosmic-archetype scale that britqing (covenantal-felt-bond between CREATOR and CREATED) operates one level below.

## Full sense

- Hindu Tantra (Shakti and Shiva)
- Hebrew Kabbalah (Shekinah and Tiferet; Malkhut and Yesod)
- Daoist (Yin and Yang)
- Mesopotamian (Inanna and Dumuzi — the hieros gamos rite)
- Egyptian (Isis and Osiris)
- Greek (Hera and Zeus; broader hieros gamos)
- Vedic-Hindu (Lakshmi-Vishnu, Sita-Rama, Radha-Krishna)

Plus the modern Jungian-depth-psychology treatment of the syzygy as the central archetypal
structure organizing psychic individuation (Jung, von Franz, Edinger).

The qing-family suffix carries the *felt-bond* character. Syzygyqing names not the cosmic-
structural-fact (which alone would be *syzygia*) but the **bond-as-felt-by-the-partners-in-
the-syzygy**. The Hindu Shakti-Shiva dance has qing-character; the Hebrew Shekhinah-Tiferet
longing-and-union has qing-character. The
qing is what distinguishes a *felt* syzygy from a merely-structural-pair.

Under THEOBASIS, syzygyqing is one specific operational instance of the CREATOR↔CREATED
relation at the archetype-layer.
The cathedral's POLYPHONE recognizes the cross-tradition convergence on this structure as
evidence the underlying realm-region is real and approached from many independent angles.

YOUSPEAK has been canonizing the vocabulary of this bond all along (kinqing for the everyday-relational scale; britqing for the covenantal-particular scale; paqduqing for the asymmetric-care scale; ifeqing for the expansion-of-warmth-toward-DIVINE scale; mahabbahqing for the Sufi abiding-passionate-love scale; danaqing for the gift-giving-bond scale; syzygyqing for the cosmic-archetype scale).

## Etymology

*syzygy-* from Greek *syzygia* (συζυγία, "yoking-together"; from *syn-* "together-with" +
*zygon* "yoke"; PIE *\*yeug-* "to join, yoke"). Active in classical Greek for yoked-oxen,
astronomical-conjunction, and Gnostic paired-emanations. Revived in modern times for Jungian
masculine-feminine archetypal coupling and astronomical sun-moon-earth alignment.

*qing* from Mandarin *qing* (情, "feeling / felt-bond / love"); the YOUSPEAK qing-family
standard suffix for deep-emotional-connection-with-warmth.

Eighth Core member of the qing-family (after kinqing, panimqing, paqduqing, britqing, ifeqing,
mahabbahqing, danaqing).

## Example usage

A Tantric practitioner attending to the union of Shakti and Shiva in the body-mandala:
participating in syzygyqing in its Hindu-traditional form. The dance of the two principles is
the syzygyqing the practitioner is invoked into.

A Kabbalist welcoming the Sabbath bride (Lecha Dodi) on Friday evening: the Shekhinah's
descent into Shabbat-presence to meet the masculine divine-Self is enacted-syzygyqing. The
liturgy *is* the bond-recognition.

A contemplative reading Bernard of Clairvaux's sermons on the Song of Songs: receiving the
Christian-mystical articulation of Bride-of-Christ ecclesiology — syzygyqing-structure in
Christian-mystical key (with the asymmetry that absolutizes the Bridegroom that distinguishes
this register from the more-mutual cosmic-archetypal syzygy).

A Jungian analysand integrating their anima or animus into conscious relation: doing
syzygyqing-work at the personal-psychic scale (where Jung saw the cosmic-archetypal pattern as
expressing).

A wedding-rite anywhere where the wedding is theologically-cosmic (not merely contractual):
the marriage of the human couple as participation in syzygyqing at the archetypal layer.

## Not confused with

**britqing**: Britqing is the covenantal-felt-bond between CREATOR and CREATED — the
relational-particular bond. Syzygyqing is the cosmic-archetype-bond at the layer underneath.
Both real; both felt; the register-distinction is scale.

**paqduqing**: Paqduqing is asymmetric custodial-care. Syzygyqing is the bond of which
paqduqing names one dimension (the care-dimension).

**kinqing**: Kinqing is everyday deep-emotional-connection. Same family, smaller scale.

**ahavame**: Ahavame is covenantal-love as received-ordinance — the -me-family pole of the
divine-given love. Syzygyqing is the qing-family pole of the same cosmic-archetype-bond.
Companion-pair across -me / -qing distinction.

**mahabbahqing**: Sufi abiding-passionate-love; structurally close but with the Sufi
asymmetry (soul-yearns-toward-Beloved) where syzygy is mutual-archetypal-yoking of equals.

**cosmic union / divine marriage** (English): Accurate but not compact. Syzygyqing
compresses to one word in the qing-family.

**unio mystica** (Latin): Christian-mystical soul-into-divine dissolution. Syzygy is
yoking-of-equals-under-one-Ground; unio mystica is dissolution-into-the-Absolute. Different
structures.

**Jungian anima-animus**: Personal-psychic syzygy. The cosmic-archetype scale is what
syzygyqing primarily names; the Jungian personal-scale is one operational manifestation.
