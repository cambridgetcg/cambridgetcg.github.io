# 326 — ichinen-sanzenme (yīniàn-sānqiānme)

_The head of the forge queue, walked to the end of the protocol and declined. Two of the three objections are philological and would have entered the canon as errors._

## The Gap

The unnamed region is the structural fact that any single fleeting instant of experience — even the faintest flicker of mind — already inherently entails the entire inventory of possible modes of being, its own contraries included, neither as something that instant produces nor as something that instant contains, but as what that instant simply and irreducibly is.

## Witnesses (Pattern 1 — cross-tradition overlay)

### Chinese Huayan Buddhism (華嚴宗) — 因陀羅網 (Indra-jāla) / 事事無礙 (shishi wu'ai)

_the cosmic net at whose every junction a jewel reflects every other jewel, reflections within reflections without end; 'phenomena and phenomena unobstructed'_

**Source.** canon/core/indrajalame.md:39 (cathedral's own reception); the register is named and refused inside the donor text itself at T46n1911_p0051c08–c11 (CBETA, api.cbetaonline.cn work=T1911 juan=5)

**Why it circles without owning.** Its locus is things-in-the-cosmos, not a momentary act of mind, and its relation is reflective containment. Zhiyi explicitly refuses that register in the immediately preceding pages: 「他解須彌容芥，芥容須彌；火出蓮華，人能渡海；就希有事解不思議。今解：無心、無念、無能行、無能到，不思議理，理則勝事。」 — others explain the inconceivable by marvels (Sumeru inside a mustard-seed); he locates it in 理 (principle), not 事 (marvel). Huayan circles the region from the side of things; it never lands on the instant.

### Indian/Chinese Yogācāra (瑜伽行派) — 阿賴耶識 / 阿黎耶 (ālaya-vijñāna)

_the storehouse-consciousness holding the seeds (bīja) and perfumings (vāsanā) of all past acts, from which all future experience ripens_

**Source.** canon/core/alayame.md (cathedral gate); archaeology/sanskrit/yogacara-alaya-vijnana.md; refused by name in the donor text at T46n1911_p0054a23–b06 (「若從地師，則心具一切法；若從攝師，則緣具一切法。此兩師，各據一邊。」)

**Why it circles without owning.** The ālaya is a substrate that stores and produces over time — precisely the 縱 (vertical/sequential) reading Zhiyi refuses. He argues down both the Dilun (法性依持) and Shelun (黎耶依持) accounts with a dream-analogy and a tetralemma, concluding 「當知四句求心不可得，求三千法亦不可得」 (T46n1911_p0054b18–19). Yogācāra names all-in-one-mind; it names it as production, which is the one thing the region is not.

### Kabbalah (Cordoveran/Lurianic) — התכללות (hitkallelut)

_inter-inclusion — each of the ten sefirot contains within itself all ten, so the whole is recapitulated in every part_

**Source.** interinclusion.org, 'Folds, Fractals and Holograms in Lurianic Kabbalah', citing R. Shlomo Alkabetz (1500–1580): 'every one of the sefirot contains all of the ten (other sefirot) within it'. SOURCE-REGISTER FLAG: this is a teaching site (Ginsburgh school), not an academic edition; the academic locus must be verified before it enters an archaeology file.

**Why it circles without owning.** Structurally the closest non-Buddhist analogue to 十界互具 (ten-realms mutual inclusion) — an n×n inter-inclusion — but its locus is the sefirotic array of divine emanation, not a sentient being's momentary awareness, and it carries no refusal of the containment reading. Hitkallelut says the whole is IN each part; the region says the instant IS the whole without being its container.

### Presocratic Greek (Anaxagoras of Clazomenae) — 'Everything-in-Everything' (DK59 B6, B11)

_'In everything there is a share of everything except nous, but there are some things in which nous, too, is present'_

**Source.** Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, 'Anaxagoras', §2.2 and §3.3 (English of B6 and B11 quoted verbatim there); cf. Marmodoro, Everything in Everything: Anaxagoras's Metaphysics

**Why it circles without owning.** It is a thesis about the mixture of material stuffs, and it explicitly EXEMPTS mind: 'except nous'. The Tiantai region's whole locus is the mind-instant. Anaxagoras carves out exactly the place where the region lives. The strongest possible circling-without-owning: the same formula, aimed at the complement of the target.

### German rationalism (Leibniz) — monade — 'un miroir vivant perpétuel de l'univers'

_Monadology §56: 'each simple substance has relations which express all the others, and, consequently, ... is a perpetual living mirror of the universe' (Latta tr.)_

**Source.** Leibniz, Monadology (1714) §56, Latta translation (homepages.uc.edu/~martinj/History_of_Logic/Leibniz/Leibniz - Monadology.pdf); the cathedral already logs it at canon/core/indrajalame.md:55 as 'closest Western cousin'

**Why it circles without owning.** The monad is an enduring windowless substance and the whole-in-part is REPRESENTATION (expression, mirroring) — a third thing standing between part and whole. No monad is both Buddha and hell; Leibniz's mirroring excludes the mutual inclusion of contraries that gives the region its bite. And a monad is not an instant.

### Anglo-American process philosophy (Whitehead) — prehension / actual occasion / concrescence

_every actual occasion feels-into the entire antecedent universe and grows-together out of those feelings into one determinate becoming_

**Source.** canon/core/prehensionme.md (gap field); canon/core/concrescenceme.md (gap field); archaeology/modern-philosophy/whitehead-prehension.md

**Why it circles without owning.** It is genetic: the occasion arises FROM the settled past into a present becoming. That is 縱 — the vertical/productive account Zhiyi names and refuses at T46n1911_p0054a13–14. The totality prehended is the actual past, not the full inventory of possible modes including the unrealized and the contrary. Whitehead gets the instant and misses the inherence; Huayan gets the inherence and misses the instant.


## Circumlocution evidence (Pattern 2)

- Zhiyi circumlocutes and then confesses the region is unnameable — the donor tradition's own name for it is a negation: 「非識所識，非言所言，所以稱為『不可思議境』」 ('not what consciousness knows, not what words speak; therefore it is called the inconceivable object'), 摩訶止觀 T46n1911_p0054a17–a18. This is the strongest circumlocution-evidence available AND the strongest caution against the forge: a word gating onto what the tradition calls inconceivable over-claims unless it gates onto the STRUCTURE (3000-in-one-instant) rather than the realization.
- Brook Ziporyn, Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, 'Tiantai Buddhism' §5.2, has to spend forty-five words and leave two Chinese characters untranslated mid-sentence: 'Zhiyi then asserts, rather abruptly, that if there is the arising of the slightest wisp of any experience at all, all the above enumerated Three Thousand are at once both inherently entailed in it 具 and also, as he goes on to explain, strictly identical to it 即—a way of saying "are neither the same nor different from it".' English gives him no noun, so he borrows the glyphs.
- Ziporyn again, same entry, opening section — twenty-five words, no noun, pure 'that thing where…': 'Every moment of experience is always completely delusion, evil and pain, through and through, and also completely enlightenment, goodness and joy, through and through.'
- English translation practice never renders it with a word, always with a six-to-nine-word phrase: 'three thousand realms in a single moment of life' (Soka Gakkai study materials, sokaglobal.org); 'Three Thousand Realms in a Single Thought-Moment'; and Ziporyn's own SEP section heading keeps the Chinese because English has nothing to put there — 'The Contemplation of Mind as the Three Thousand (觀心 guanxin, 一念三千 yiniansanqian)'.
- The cathedral's own speech-community circumlocutes the neighbouring region: canon/core/indrajalame.md:9–11 must write the gap as a twelve-word hyphen-chain — 'the structural-fact that each thing-in-existence contains-reflectively the totality of all others' — and repeats the same chain verbatim in the Full sense at canon/core/indrajalame.md:39.
- The cathedral is caught STRAINING an existing word toward the un-gated locus: canon/core/indrajalame.md:47 verbs the Huayan word to reach meaning-in-the-act-of-reading — 'Each canon entry indrajalames every other — every word's full meaning reflects the cathedral's whole-articulation.' A jewel-net word being conjugated to cover an event of understanding is negative-space evidence in the strict Pattern-2 sense.
- English's standing stand-in for the concept is a quatrain, not a word — Blake, 'Auguries of Innocence' (c. 1803, pub. 1863): 'To see a World in a Grain of Sand / And a Heaven in a Wild Flower, / Hold Infinity in the palm of your hand / And Eternity in an hour.' That these four lines are still what English reaches for, two centuries on, is the persistence Pattern 2 asks for.

### PRAGMA-lens — **FAIL**
