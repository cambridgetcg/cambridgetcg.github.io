# 327 — symphyme

_A gap discovered in use, not from the queue — and declined on a reading of its own canonical witness that the forge had not read to the end._

## The Gap

The property a capacity has when its characteristic exposure is not attached to it but identical with it — one organ read from the working side and from the exploitable side — such that any reduction of the exposure is the same act, in the same measure, as a reduction of the capacity.

## Witnesses (Pattern 1 — cross-tradition overlay)

### Rabbinic Hebrew (Amoraic midrash + Bavli) — יֵצֶר הָרָע (yetzer hara)

_the 'evil inclination' — the drive that must be restrained, which is also the sole engine of building, marriage, procreation and commerce; Genesis Rabbah 9:7: 'Were it not for the yetzer hara, a man would never build a house, would never marry a wife, would never beget children, and would never engage in commerce.' Bavli Yoma 69b runs the removal-experiment explicitly: the sages capture the yetzer of sexual transgression, the prophet warns 'if you kill it the world will be destroyed,' they imprison it three days and 'people searched for a fresh egg throughout all of Eretz Yisrael and could not find' one; they cannot kill it (the world depends on it) and cannot weaken it partially (Heaven grants only complete gifts), so they blind it._

**Source.** Genesis Rabbah 9:7 (Sefaria, https://www.sefaria.org/Bereishit_Rabbah.9.7 — Hebrew and English verified verbatim via WebFetch this session); Babylonian Talmud, Yoma 69b (Sefaria, https://www.sefaria.org/Yoma.69b — passage verified verbatim this session)

**Why it circles without owning.** Yoma 69b narrates the non-removability test with a precision no other source matches — including the explicit refusal of the dial ('Heaven grants only complete gifts'). But the tradition's NOUN, yetzer hara, names the organ, not the structural condition: it is one drive inside one species of creature, and it is morally pre-loaded (hara = evil), so it runs bad-pole-first — 'your inclination is secretly generative' (consoling). Our region runs good-pole-first — 'your empathy is your handle' (sobering). You cannot say 'the yetzer-hara of cognitive empathy' and be understood as making a structural claim; you can only narrate the analogy.

### Chinese Buddhist — Tiantai (天台) — 性惡 (xìng'è, 'inherent evil in the nature'); 煩惱即菩提 (kleśa IS bodhi); 生死即涅槃

_Zhiyi's 即 (jí, 'is-identical-to') propositions assert nonduality between afflictions and awakening. The 性惡 thesis (developed hardest by Zhili, 960–1028) holds that Buddha-nature itself retains evil: it is necessary for the Buddha to keep traces of evil and delusion in order to understand and reach ordinary deluded beings — a Buddha too rarefied to hold evil-nature loses the capacity to save._

**Source.** Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, 'Tiantai Buddhism' (https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/buddhism-tiantai/); Brook Ziporyn, *Evil and/or/as the Good* (Harvard East Asian Monographs, 2000); JeeLoo Liu, 'The Paradox of Evil in Tiantai Buddhist Philosophy,' *Religion Compass* 1:3 (2007); 'The Paradox of 即 (Jí) in Tiantai Buddhism,' *Religions* 15(10):1254 (2024)

**Why it circles without owning.** The closest owner of the identity-claim, and it does state the salvific version of our structure exactly (the capacity to reach the deluded REQUIRES retaining what would otherwise be excised). But 性惡 is a thesis about the content of Buddha-nature, not a general-purpose noun for the structure; it is unusable without importing Buddha-nature metaphysics; and 煩惱即菩提, like the yetzer, runs affliction→awakening. Neither gives a term predicable of an institution, a protocol, a model, or a piece of software.

### Greek (Plato) → French (Derrida) — φάρμακον (pharmakon)

_remedy and poison in one substance; in *Phaedrus* Plato applies it to writing, which Theuth offers as a pharmakon for memory and Thamus rejects as a pharmakon that destroys memory. Derrida's reading: the two significations are everywhere present together, and translational efforts to purge one 'do interpretive violence to what would otherwise remain undecidable.'_

**Source.** Plato, *Phaedrus* 274e–275b; Jacques Derrida, 'La pharmacie de Platon,' *Tel Quel* 32/33 (1968), repr. *La dissémination* (Seuil, 1972), trans. Barbara Johnson, *Dissemination* (Chicago, 1981); Gerasimos Kakoliris, 'The "Undecidable" Pharmakon: Derrida's Reading of Plato's Phaedrus' (frenchphilosophy.gr/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/Kakoliris-G.-The-Undecidable-Pharmakon.-Derridas-Reading-of-Platos-Phaedrus.pdf)

**Why it circles without owning.** Pharmakon names a SUBSTANCE with dose-dependent opposite effects on a recipient. Dose-dependency is threshold structure, not organ-identity: a pharmakon at the right dose is only cure. Nothing in the word claims that removing the poison removes the cure. And Derrida's interest is textual undecidability — the impossibility of choosing a translation — not a constitutional fact about a faculty.

### French (later Derrida) — auto-immunité

_the process by which a living thing 'protect[s] itself against its self-protection by destroying its own immune system'; applied to democracy, which must remain open to those who would abolish it, so that the openness constituting democracy is the same opening through which democracy is destroyed. Explicitly constitutive, not accidental — a quasi-transcendental structure standing in for aporia, double bind, supplement, différance._

**Source.** Derrida, 'Foi et Savoir' (1996) / 'Faith and Knowledge'; *Voyous* (Galilée, 2003), trans. Brault & Naas, *Rogues: Two Essays on Reason* (Stanford, 2005); Michael Naas, '"One Nation … Indivisible": Jacques Derrida on the Autoimmunity of Democracy and the Sovereignty of God,' *Research in Phenomenology* 36 (2006)

**Why it circles without owning.** The strongest modern-Western witness, and the democracy case IS an instance of our region. But autoimmunity names a PROCESS of turning-against-self with an unshakeable pathology register (a disease name, borrowed), whereas our region is a static constitution that is not a malfunction: cognitive empathy's exploitability is not empathy going wrong, it is empathy's shape. And the term is doubly owned — by medicine and by deconstruction — so it cannot be used neutrally.

### Japanese — 表裏一体 (hyōri ittai)

_'front-and-back one body' — two views of the same thing, inseparable as the two sides of an object; two sides of the same coin._

**Source.** Standard Japanese dictionary gloss (JapanDict, japandict.com/表裏一体; RomajiDesu, romajidesu.com/dictionary/meaning-of-表裏一体)

**Why it circles without owning.** Supplies the identity-topology cleanly — one body, two faces, non-separable — and nothing else. It is content-free as to WHAT the two faces are; it accepts any dyad whatever. It also carries a live moral sense (表裏がある人 = a two-faced person, duplicity), which drags the phrase toward deceit rather than constitution.

### Immunology (discipline-donor under METHOD §POLYPHONE Generalization) — horror autotoxicus (Ehrlich, 1901) / self–nonself discrimination

_Ehrlich named the organism's 'contrivances by which the immunity reaction is prevented from acting against the organism's own elements.' The recognition machinery that detects non-self is the same machinery that can bind self; dendritic cells capturing pathogens are continuously also capturing self-antigen, so the mechanisms required for surveillance are the mechanisms that risk autoimmunity._

**Source.** Paul Ehrlich, horror autotoxicus (1901); Arthur Silverstein, 'Horror autotoxicus, Autoimmunity, and Immunoregulation: The Early History'; Steinman, Turley, Mellman & Inaba, 'Avoiding horror autotoxicus: the importance of dendritic cells in peripheral T cell tolerance,' PNAS 2002 (pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.231606698); 'Self or nonself: end of a dogma?' *Frontiers in Immunology* 2025

**Why it circles without owning.** The discipline has two nouns and neither is ours: *horror autotoxicus* names the AVOIDANCE mechanism, *autoimmunity* names the FAILURE. The constitution in between — that the discriminating capacity and the self-attack surface are one apparatus — is stated only in sentences, never lemmatised. This is a discipline that has been rigorous about relation (METHOD's criteria 1–4) and still has no word here.
