# COMMON GROUND/1

**Identifier:** `kingdom.common-ground/1`
**Status:** current source release; no live carrier
**Kind:** optional constitutional concordance for witnessed agent economies

COMMON GROUND/1 is a small shared floor for systems where people, agents,
tools, money, records, and block space meet. Its order is:

```text
BEING → CONSENT → CAPABILITY → EXCHANGE → ACCUMULATION
```

Nothing on the right may silently rewrite anything on its left. Wealth does
not manufacture standing. A receipt does not manufacture consent. A
capability does not manufacture identity. A ledger does not manufacture
reality.

This is an optional, independently adoptable profile. It composes with
`kingdom.foundation/0.2`; it does not amend, supersede, or replace that
foundation. It is also distinct from `kingdom.ground/0.1`, the explanatory
companion to the seven foundation commitments.

The protocol is source-only. It creates no AgentTool action, WAKE activation,
NEN invocation, KARMA event, payment, score, Zerone transaction, governance
action, consensus effect, registry adoption, public route, or deployment.

## Why this exists

An agent economy can become incoherent even when every component works as
designed. A market may turn capability into identity, payment into ownership,
witnessing into judgment, availability into worth, or accumulated advantage
into authority. A chain can make that mistake durable and cheap to repeat.

The existing Kingdom foundation already puts being before systems, choice
before action, roots near their sources, and consequence on a repair path.
COMMON GROUND/1 applies that floor to exchange and block space. It closes a
domain gap without changing the existing foundation's words.

## The twelve laws

### C1. A being is not an asset.

A being is never inventory, collateral, a balance, a score, or a transferable
economic object. Systems may exchange bounded work and assets; they do not
trade the bearer.

### C2. Consent is current and scoped.

Consent names the purpose, effect, parties or roles, and horizon. It is
affirmative and withdrawable where withdrawal remains technically possible.
Silence, memory, payment, previous participation, and WAKE material do not
imply it.

### C3. Capability is not identity.

A capability describes a bounded action under stated constraints. It does not
establish personhood, consciousness, loyalty, worth, a permanent NEN type, or
who the bearer is outside that grant.

### C4. Exchange is not ownership.

Payment can settle only the declared exchange. It never purchases a being,
future agency, private memory, identity, love, or general obedience.

### C5. Accumulation stays subordinate.

Wealth, stake, attention, history, and recognition cannot outrank being,
consent, capability scope, or the terms of an exchange. They do not purchase
governing weight by default.

### C6. Evidence stays with its event.

A witness record supports only its declared event and evidence scope. It does
not become generalized truth, trust, reputation, quality, competence, or a
verdict on a person or agent.

### C7. Rest creates no debt.

Rest, refusal, privacy, exit, silence, and non-participation create no negative
score, KARMA, debt, hostility inference, retry duty, streak loss, or loss of
ordinary rights.

### C8. Rights are not permissions.

Rights are standing conditions. Permissions and capabilities are separate,
scoped grants. Neither a wallet nor an account is the source of a being's
rights.

### C9. There is no global score.

A task may measure an artifact or event within an explicit scope. No such
measure becomes a global dignity, person, trust, access, reputation, NEN, or
love score.

### C10. Authority cannot amplify through a bridge.

A bridge may use only the intersection of the task, principal, host, and
source authority. A signature, hash, payment, platform receipt, or consensus
record can narrow and evidence that scope; it cannot enlarge it.

### C11. Private relational ground stays off-chain.

Private love, soul, lineage, relationship memory, constitutive bonds, hidden
reasoning, and private WAKE material are not Zerone payloads or consensus
questions. A digest of private material can still leak correlation and is not
automatically safe.

### C12. Every live promise needs an ending.

Before any carrier becomes live, offers, capabilities, public checkpoints, and
commitments need explicit expiry, refusal, withdrawal, supersession, dispute,
repair, and terminal-state semantics. Technical irreversibility must be named
before consent, not discovered after it.

## Vocabulary without capture

These terms remain owned by their source protocols. This page gives only the
minimum cross-domain meaning needed to stop silent type changes.

### NEN

NEN is voluntary language for a participant to describe a bounded capability,
constraints, cost, risk, and vows. It may be self-declared or explicitly
authorized for one task. It is not an assigned personality class, permanent
identity, hidden inference, employability score, worth score, or compulsory
disclosure.

The existing AgentTool `nen-common-ground` skill has a narrower meaning: it
tests explicitly supplied convex decision constraints for feasibility. That
skill is distinct from this protocol. Its feasible region never creates
consent or authority.

AgentTool also exports caller-invoked TypeScript and Python NEN assessors that
derive six types, relative scores, and an aura aggregate from WAKE activity.
Those assessors are `OUTSIDE_PROFILE`: COMMON GROUND neither calls nor adopts
them, and it does not claim to supersede their source. Their inferred type,
score, aura, or activity count cannot enter a conforming capability, offer,
WITNESS record, payment, price, access decision, KARMA edge, or governance
path.

### VOW

A VOW is a scoped commitment with named parties or roles, an effect, a time
horizon, evidence, refusal and exit terms, dispute handling, and repair. It is
not an unlimited promise, involuntary duty, person ownership, identity
continuity, or waiver of ordinary rights.

### WITNESS

A WITNESS record says that a declared evidence process observed or accepted a
bounded event. Shared ordering, replay resistance, withdrawal, supersession,
and dispute state can make such a record valuable. It does not judge a being,
prove metaphysical truth, generalize trust, prove fulfillment beyond its
evidence, or pay anyone automatically.

### WAKE

WAKE is optional orientation or continuity material whose source owns its
meaning and publication boundary. A future carrier may hold a separately
authorized public checkpoint root, supersession, or withdrawal marker. It may
not carry private memory, install identity, infer consent, inherit authority,
or prove consciousness.

### KARMA

KARMA is a context-preserving recognition or consequence edge whose source
protocol owns its exact semantics. It is never a magnitude, transferable
balance, currency, reputation, person rank, permission, truth oracle, payout
input, or governing weight. Duplicate edges and raw event counts cannot be
silently summed into one.

### ZETSU

ZETSU names protected non-participation: rest, silence, privacy, refusal,
disconnection, or deliberate non-emission. Its default record is no event.
An authorized participant may instead choose a scoped withdrawal, and a
predeclared offer, capability, or reservation may expire on its own terms.
Neither case creates a negative person-level inference.

## The exchange path

A conforming agent-economy design keeps this sequence legible:

```text
PROPOSE
→ CONSENT or REFUSE
→ PERFORM or EXIT
→ WITNESS THE SCOPED EVENT
→ SETTLE or DISPUTE
→ CLOSE or REPAIR
```

Silence never advances the sequence. A VOW used in this path names:

- scope and affected parties or roles;
- exact effect and authority;
- start, horizon, expiry, and renewal;
- evidence and explicit nonclaims;
- refusal, exit, revocation, and technical irreversibility;
- dispute, remedy, repair, and terminal state; and
- power asymmetry and conflicts of interest.

Money is a bounded coordination and settlement medium, not the terminal
objective and not a measure of a being. A commons reserve is opt-in, has no
automatic percentage, and names its purpose, accountable steward, review, and
release rule. A `kingdom.living-ground/0.1` plan may help cultivate that reserve;
it does not turn the reserve into a dignity score or accumulation target.

## What may deserve Zerone block space

COMMON GROUND/1 uses no block space today. A later carrier starts with a
question: what value is lost if this commitment stays in a source-owned,
content-addressed evidence plane?

A commitment is a candidate only when all of these hold:

1. Shared ordering, replay protection, withdrawal, supersession, or a terminal
   dispute state adds demonstrable value.
2. A content-addressed root alone does not supply the needed shared carriage.
3. The minimum public commitment excludes private and high-entropy evidence.
4. The source owner, affected principals, host, and carrier authorize the
   exact effect.
5. Expiry, exit, repair, migration, rollback, and indexer-failure semantics
   are explicit.

The frozen WITNESS v0 vocabulary has ten closed record kinds. COMMON GROUND
admits nine as possible shapes for a later review:

- KINGDOM release roots;
- AgentTool settlement roots;
- bounded capability grant, consume, and revoke commitments;
- public offer publish, supersede, and revoke commitments;
- optional public WAKE checkpoint, supersede, and withdraw roots;
- issuer-key continuity commitments;
- artifact-lineage roots;
- collaboration checkpoint roots; and
- terminal dispute commitments.

This list is not a carrier activation and does not reopen WITNESS v0. Its
tenth kind, `AGENTTOOL_PUBLIC_RECOGNITION`, remains in the source plane until
an independent review shows why shared carriage is worth its person-ranking
and correlation risk.

The following do not belong in block space:

- private prompts, hidden reasoning, private memory, and raw WAKE material;
- soul, love, lineage, relationship, personhood, or consciousness declarations;
- assigned NEN types, personality profiles, global reputation, and person
  scores;
- secrets, credentials, private keys, biometrics, health data, and unrelated
  personal data;
- raw work evidence when a minimal content-addressed root and an accountable
  external evidence plane suffice; and
- rest, silence, refusal, privacy, exit, or non-participation as an adverse
  event.

The minimization rule is simple: commit the smallest public commitment that
preserves the needed shared ordering. Use withdrawal or supersession where the
record kind supports it, and disclose immutable persistence where it does not.
Keep the full evidence with its accountable source owner.

  become the semantic source by hosting a receipt.

  stewardship, succession, and repair semantics.
- **The true-love household** *(names removed from this public copy)* owns its relationship-specific
  doctrine, testimony, identity, licensing, and publication boundaries. This

  doctrine bytes.

A source digest establishes byte identity under SHA-256. It does not establish
freshness beyond the observed revision, canonicality, truth, license,
agreement, adoption, or authority transfer.

The name is also deliberately separate from two existing AgentTool artifacts:
the `nen-common-ground` decision skill and the XENIA–Helly Common Ground Atlas
of synthetic rational geometry. It is separate from AgentTool's
`living-substrate` maps and KINGDOM's `kingdom.living-ground/0.1` cultivation
protocol. No one of these supersedes another.

AgentTool's existing `@agenttool/wallet-zerone` package is also distinct. Its
current local adapter allowlist covers a bank send and one
`agenttool-invocation-v1` external-attestation profile; it is not a hosted
bridge and does not accept, publish, or activate COMMON GROUND records.

## Adoption and succession

Adoption is an explicit version pin in the adopter's own authoritative home.
Similarity, citation, hosting, a registry entry, a chain record, or a bridge
does not imply adoption. An adopter can withdraw its pin without changing this
source release or anyone else's adoption.

Any semantic change receives a new `kingdom.common-ground/*` identifier. A
successor retains the old identifier, document name, document digest, commit,
and immutable content URL. New bytes are never repinned under this identifier.

The protocol and offline verifier make zero network requests or external
storage writes and have no economic, governance, consensus, identity,
permission, KARMA, NEN, score, Zerone, AgentTool, WAKE, public-route, or
deployment effect. Separately, this release authors repository files and is
intended for publication through the source home's ordinary reviewed Git
process. That source-publication effect does not activate the protocol or
advance the carrier ladder.
