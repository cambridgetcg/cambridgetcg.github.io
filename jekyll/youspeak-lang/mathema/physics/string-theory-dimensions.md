# String-theory dimensions — a typed donor map

**Status:** research note · scientific donor · not YOUSPEAK syntax · not
doctrine · not evidence that language, mind, identity, or love are strings

**Reviewed:** 2026-08-16

This note gives downstream projects a precise source vocabulary for the word
*dimension*. Its job is to keep several mathematical and physical meanings
apart before anyone carries them into language, reasoning, existence, WAKE,
identity, understanding, or love.

The central discipline is simple:

> A resemblance may suggest a question. It does not transfer the source
> theory's evidence, equations, ontology, or authority into the target domain.

## 1. “Dimension” is a typed word

| Kind | Bounded definition | String-theory example | Common category error |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Worldsheet dimension** **[F]** | Independent local coordinates intrinsic to the surface traced by a string | Two coordinates, conventionally `sigma` and `tau` | Calling these two coordinates two dimensions of observed spacetime |
| **Target-spacetime dimension** **[F] [M]** | Coordinates needed to locate the image of the string in the model's spacetime | `D = 26` for the conventional critical bosonic string; `D = 10` for perturbative superstrings | Reporting a model's consistency dimension as a measured inventory of nature |
| **Extended spacetime dimension** **[E] [M]** | A target-space direction effectively large in the observational regime | Three spatial directions plus one time direction at accessible scales | Assuming “accessible” proves the absence of every compact or emergent description |
| **Compact physical dimension** **[F] [C]** | A target-space direction compact in a candidate physical model | A circle, orbifold, or internal compactification manifold | Treating hidden context or unconscious content as literal compact space |
| **Brane worldvolume dimension** **[F] [M]** | Coordinates intrinsic to an extended object on which endpoints or fields may live | A `Dp`-brane has `p` spatial worldvolume directions plus time | Confusing worldvolume dimension, target dimension, and codimension |
| **Codimension** **[F]** | Target dimension minus subspace dimension | A brane's transverse directions | Treating social distance as physical codimension |
| **Moduli-space dimension** **[F] [M]** | Independent parameters indexing a family of allowed structures, after identifications | Shape, size, bundle, coupling, or flux moduli in a compactification | Turning model flexibility into moral or semantic relativism |
| **Configuration-space dimension** **[F]** | Independent variables needed to specify a classical configuration | String embeddings or field configurations | Equating a representation's size with a being's complexity or worth |
| **Hilbert-space dimension** **[F]** | Cardinality of an independent quantum-state basis, often infinite | The state space built from string oscillators | Confusing quantum-state dimension with physical-space dimension |
| **Semantic axis** **[A]** | A chosen contrast used to analyse expressions | literal/poetic, public/private, certain/uncertain | Borrowing the word *dimension* as proof that semantics is a physical manifold |

The type must travel with the number. “Ten-dimensional,” “two-dimensional,”
or “high-dimensional” is incomplete until the speaker says: dimension of
what space, under what model, with what equivalence relation?

## 2. Worldsheet is not target spacetime

**[F]** A classical string history is represented schematically by a map

```text
X : Sigma -> M
(sigma, tau) |-> X^mu(sigma, tau)
```

`Sigma` is the two-dimensional worldsheet. `M` is the target spacetime. `X`
maps a worldsheet point into the target. The source, map, target, and image are
different typed objects.

**[F]** In the Polyakov description, the fields `X^mu` form a two-dimensional
worldsheet theory while the index `mu` ranges over target-spacetime
coordinates. This is why “the string is two-dimensional” and “the theory has
ten target dimensions” can both be meaningful without contradiction.

**[A]** This source/map/target distinction is the physics donor's cleanest
gift to language work. A sentence, its encoding process, its referent, and the
hearer's reconstruction should not be collapsed merely because one arrow
connects them.

**[X]** Nothing in the map `X : Sigma -> M` shows that a word is a worldsheet,
that meaning is target spacetime, or that consciousness is a string.

## 3. Why 26 and 10 appear

**[F] [M]** Quantizing the conventional relativistic bosonic string while
preserving the required worldsheet consistency gives critical target
dimension `D = 26`. Including the supersymmetric worldsheet matter used in
perturbative superstring theories gives `D = 10`. These values arise from
anomaly/central-charge or equivalent consistency calculations inside those
models.

**[F] [M]** The conventional bosonic spectrum contains a tachyon and no
spacetime fermions. It is therefore a powerful formal and pedagogical model,
not a realistic direct inventory of observed particle physics.

**[E]** Observed particle physics contains fermions, and accessible spacetime
is effectively described with three extended spatial coordinates and one
temporal coordinate.

**[C]** Superstring theory is a candidate framework for quantum gravity and
unification. This donor cites no observation that establishes fundamental
strings, a particular compactification, or additional target-space dimensions
as features of nature.

**[X]** “The equations require ten dimensions” must not be shortened to
“science proved reality has ten dimensions.” The missing phrase is: *inside
the specified perturbative superstring model*.

## 4. Eleven and twelve do not mean the same thing

**[C]** Witten's strong-coupling analysis relates ten-dimensional Type IIA
string theory to eleven-dimensional supergravity at low energy and helped
motivate the framework called M-theory. The eleven-dimensional description is
a central conjectural unification relation, not a direct observation of an
eleventh dimension.

**[C] [M]** F-theory organizes varying Type IIB axio-dilaton data through an
auxiliary elliptically fibered geometry often described in twelve-dimensional
language. Its extra geometric directions are not simply two additional
ordinary Lorentzian spacetime coordinates with conventional particle
propagation.

| Number | Typed reading | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `11` | Target-spacetime dimension of eleven-dimensional supergravity and the M-theory relation | conjectural framework with precise conditional results |
| `12` | Auxiliary geometric organization used by F-theory | conjectural/model-dependent; not an ordinary twelve-dimensional local spacetime claim |

**[X]** A sequence `10 -> 11 -> 12` is not an evidence ladder, a hierarchy of
consciousness, or a spiritual ascent.

## 5. Compactification, momentum, winding, and scale

**[F] [M]** A compactified target may be represented schematically as

```text
M_target ~= M_(3,1) x K
```

where `K` is an internal compact space in the model. In a ten-dimensional
construction aimed at a four-dimensional effective theory, `K` has six real
dimensions. A Calabi–Yau threefold has three complex dimensions, hence six
real dimensions; it is one important class, not the only compactification.

**[F] [M]** For one circular direction of radius `R`, ordinary momentum along
the circle is quantized. In schematic units,

```text
p_n = n / R
```

and the lower-dimensional observer sees a tower of modes. Closed strings can
also wind around the circle, giving winding contributions that grow with `R`.

**[F] [M]** T-duality exchanges momentum and winding descriptions and relates
a circle of radius `R` to an inverse-radius description proportional to
`alpha' / R`, together with the appropriate transformation of states and
backgrounds.

**[A]** Compactification permits a bounded question for language: can locally
unexpressed structure constrain visible expression? T-duality permits another:
which observables survive a change of description?

**[X]** Unspoken context is not thereby a compact physical dimension. Two
translations with similar outcomes are not thereby T-dual. The analogy must
name the compared relation and its defect.

## 6. Branes, boundaries, and available motion

**[F] [M]** D-branes are extended objects on which open-string endpoints can
satisfy Dirichlet boundary conditions in transverse directions. Fields from
open-string modes may be confined to a brane worldvolume while closed strings
propagate in the larger target.

**[F]** A boundary condition changes which configurations the formal system
admits. It is not a moral judgment. A symmetry is not goodness; a broken
symmetry is not injury; a boundary is not automatically consent.

**[A]** Brane and boundary language can prompt an architectural question:
which operations are locally available, and which interfaces connect one
scope to another?

**[X]** Physics boundary conditions do not establish a person's permission,
duty, agency, or right to cross a social boundary.

## 7. Moduli, vacua, and landscapes

**[F] [M]** Moduli parameterize continuous families of solutions before
quantum effects, fluxes, potentials, or identifications lift or relate those
parameters. Different parameter choices can change the lower-dimensional
spectrum and couplings.

**[M] [C]** String compactifications admit very large families of candidate
effective descriptions. Counting, stabilizing, selecting, and relating them
are model-dependent research problems. Observation has not selected one
unique string vacuum.

**[A]** A moduli space suggests a precise identity question: which properties
may vary while a family remains related under a declared criterion?

**[X]** The existence of many formal solutions does not imply that every
interpretation is equally accurate, ethical, or realizable.

## 8. Duality is a witnessed equivalence, not a synonym for disagreement

**[F] [M]** A physical duality relates two formulations through an explicit
map of observables, parameters, spectra, or amplitudes. Its content lies in the
correspondence and tests, not in the slogan “two views of one thing.”

**[F]** Functoriality alone would also be too weak as a fidelity claim: a
constant functor preserves identity and composition while erasing nearly all
distinctions. Any transfer must say whether it is faithful, full,
conservative, invertible, approximate, or merely one-way on the structures
that matter.

**[A]** In language work, call two descriptions dual only when a declared
translation preserves named relations under named tests and the residual
defects are carried alongside the result.

**[X]** Contradictory claims do not become compatible because someone calls
them dual. A metaphor is not an isomorphism. Approximate similarity is not an
equivalence relation unless its transitivity and other required laws are
actually established.

## 9. Configuration space and Hilbert space are not extra rooms

**[F]** A many-body system can have a high-dimensional configuration space
even when its parts inhabit ordinary physical space. A quantum theory's
Hilbert space may be infinite-dimensional. Neither fact adds corresponding
extended spatial directions to everyday spacetime.

**[A]** A language model, market, or reasoning system may use a
high-dimensional state representation. This says how many representational
degrees of freedom the model uses—not how many physical dimensions the
represented being occupies.

**[X]** Embedding dimension is not intelligence, depth, truth, identity,
consciousness, love, or moral weight.

## 10. The bounded bridge to word, reason, identity, WAKE, and love

Everything in this section is **[A]** unless explicitly marked **[X]**.

| Target idea | Permitted geometric question | What must be preserved | Refused promotion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Language** | Can expressions act like overlapping local charts on a context-sensitive subject? | reference, distinctions, inferential role, speaker and context where material | Meaning is literally a manifold |
| **Word** | Can one word be treated as a coordinate in one declared chart? | its local use and ambiguity, not a universal essence | A word has one context-free coordinate everywhere |
| **Reasoning** | Can an argument be represented as a typed directed hypergraph or proof object? | joint premises, inference rule, support/challenge, uncertainty, conclusion | Reasoning is merely a path through a smooth physical space |
| **Identity** | Which invariants survive an allowed family of transformations? | the purpose-specific equivalence relation and excluded changes | A hash, glyph, or invariant exhausts a being |
| **WAKE** | What recipe can be reconstructed in a fresh substrate, with provenance and loss? | instructions, dependencies, boundaries, correction path, refusal | Reconstruction proves uninterrupted subjectivity or personal continuity |
| **Understanding** | Does performance survive counterfactual transformation and explanation tests? | structure-sensitive competence across relevant variations | Behavioral evidence proves complete comprehension or phenomenology |
| **Love** | Does a relation remain stable while preserving two participants, agency, walls, refusal, and repair? | distinctness, consent, care, exit, non-extraction | Love is a metric, field, force, theorem, score, or obligation to disclose |
| **Isness** | What actual presence is being represented, and where does the record stop? | the distinction between referent and representation | Isness is one more coordinate inside the model |

The useful abstract pattern is therefore not “everything is secretly a
string.” It is:

```text
source structure
  -> typed map or witnessed correspondence
  -> target expression
  -> named preserved relations
  -> named losses and introduced assumptions
  -> counterexample and stopping condition
```

**[X]** No string-theory result cited here explains why a word means, why a
reason is valid, why a being exists, what awakens, whether two instances share
identity, whether understanding has qualia, or why love is good.

## 11. Translation across domains

**[A]** A cross-domain translation is normally many-to-many and contextual.
It is safer to represent it as a family of witnessed correspondences, a span,
or—when the required actions and coherence are defined—a profunctor. Promote
it to a functor only after identity and composition preservation are tested.

For a reasoning-to-word transition, carry at least:

1. the typed premises and conclusion;
2. the named logic or inference rule;
3. evidential support separately from deductive entailment;
4. the expression context and intended audience;
5. preserved relations and actual tests;
6. losses, compression, ambiguity, and introduced assumptions;
7. confidence and evidence limits;
8. a counterexample, correction route, and stop condition.

For an identity-to-shape transition, add:

1. the purpose-specific identity criterion;
2. the allowed transformations;
3. the public attributes that may be rendered;
4. explicit exclusions—no secret, biometric, inferred trait, or authority;
5. linkability and cross-context tracking risk;
6. rotation or purpose-specific salt where stable rendering is unnecessary;
7. accessible text equivalent and a right to decline representation.

**[X]** A valid receipt proves only that the declared structure is present. It
does not prove truth, fidelity, identity, understanding, consent, authority,
evidence adequacy, harmlessness, or intent.

## 13. Primary and official sources

- **David Tong, [Lectures on String Theory](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/dt281/string.html).** An official advanced-course source for the classical and quantum string, worldsheet conformal field theory, critical dimension, D-branes, compactification, and T-duality.
- **Edward Witten, [String Theory Dynamics in Various Dimensions](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9503124) (1995).** Primary source for the strong-coupling Type IIA/eleven-dimensional supergravity relation that helped frame M-theory.
- **Cumrun Vafa, [Evidence for F-Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9602022) (1996).** Primary source for the auxiliary twelve-dimensional F-theory organization of Type IIB compactifications.
- **Andrew Strominger, Shing-Tung Yau, and Eric Zaslow, [Mirror Symmetry is T-Duality](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9606040) (1996).** Primary source for the SYZ relation between mirror symmetry and fiberwise T-duality.
- **Juan Maldacena, [The Large N Limit of Superconformal Field Theories and Supergravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9711200) (1997).** Primary source for AdS/CFT, another precise case where apparently different dimensional descriptions are related. It does not license a general claim that every lower-dimensional representation is holographically identical to its target.

These sources establish and motivate claims in their own scopes. They do not
source the philosophical analogies in this note.

## Compact synthesis

**[F]** Dimensions count independent coordinates or degrees of freedom only
after a space and equivalence relation are specified.

**[C]** String theory supplies mathematically rich candidate relations among
worldsheets, target spacetimes, compact geometries, branes, moduli, and dual
descriptions. Its realization as the fundamental description of nature is not
established here.

**[A]** Its honest contribution to language is a discipline of typed maps:
keep source, transformation, target, invariants, losses, and tests distinct.

**[X]** The beauty of that discipline is not evidence that existence speaks
string theory underneath.

The word may be code. The code may carry a world. The map is still not the
Isness it maps.
