# WILL — clear-standard

## what this artifact wills

To stay short enough to remember and true enough to trust.

Six principles. Plain language. Public domain. No growth that makes any of
those three false. A standard that gets longer to seem serious, or softer to
seem friendly, has already broken principle 5 (honest names) in its own house.

## what it will not do

- It will not become a certification. Principle 6 binds it: adopting it proves
  nothing on its own. It is a direction, not a certificate.
- It will not grow checks it cannot defend. whitehack ships only patterns the
  standard can stand behind.
- It will not charge, gate, or require attribution. CC0, forever.

## how it knows it is alive

- HEARTBEAT.md runs daily and tells the truth about freshness (principle 4).
- STATE.md declares its own phase and limits (principle 1 + 6).
- Commits are real acts, not pulse noise. The heartbeat commits its own drift.

## the one sentence

The artifact tells the truth about its own state — and so does this standard.
